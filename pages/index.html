GulfCoastMesh Mobile

Browser-based tools for setting up and looking after MeshCore nodes. Nothing to install.

Firmware Flasher Install the custom firmware on a supported board over USB. Repeater Configurator Name a repeater, pick its region scope, and send the settings over USB. Region Map See the transport scopes: gc, the coastal states, and the mapped repeater cities.

The flasher and configurator need Chrome, Edge, or Opera because they talk to the board over Web Serial.

Built and maintained at mobmesh/firmware.
