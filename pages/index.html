What Enhanced+ Adds
  • Off-site Firmware Updates One command over the mesh. The node powers its hotspot, joins WiFi, fetches the firmware, verifies it, installs it, and comes back. About a minute.
  • Recovery Rollback New firmware runs on probation. If it fails during startup, the node reverts to the previous image and reboots itself.
  • Settings Stay Current Receive signed region and operating-setting updates from trusted publishers over the mesh. Keep participating nodes aligned without configuring each one separately.
  • Test Settings With Recovery Applies values for a set number of seconds and puts it back unless you confirm it. For the settings that can quietly take a node off the mesh.
  • Brownout Protections A repeater leaves service before the danger zone and sleeps, then returns on its own once the battery has recovered to safe levels. Prevents stranding a node that needs local reset.
  • Enhanced Clock Accuracy Correct timer and reboot-related clock issues. Trusted publishers can also repair substantially incorrect clocks over the mesh.
  • Flood Control Stop spam floods from abusing your repeater. Prevent identified spam floods from being forwarded, preserving transmit airtime for useful traffic.
  • Practical Diagnostics Get plain-language findings for battery health, neighbour links, noise and nearby interference—with a suggested next action. Supported boards can also compare reception with their external amplifier on and off.
  • Direct Packet Retries Increase direct-message delivery by 35%. When enabled, the repeater retries packets when it doesn’t hear the next hop forward them.

Features vary by board and device role.

MeshCore: stock vs Enhanced+

MeshCore Enhanced+ builds on upstream MeshCore with remote maintenance, signed settings updates, spam flood control and practical diagnostics. The comparison below highlights the added capabilities.

Situation Stock MeshCore MeshCore Enhanced+
Updating a remote node* Local upload through the node's WiFi access point Trigger a download over an existing WiFi connection or hotspot with a mesh command
New firmware fails during startup* May require manual recovery Boots the previous image automatically if the startup trial fails
Region or operating settings change Configure each node individually The repeater can stay current with regional settings by responding to updates via the mesh
Trying a risky setting Temporary trials for radio parameters Timed trials for radio parameters, transmit power and other supported settings; reverts unless confirmed
Low battery May brown out as the battery runs flat Configurable safe sleep and automatic return after the battery recovers
An unset or drifting clock May need the clock set again after a reboot Timer fixes and signed mesh time updates repair substantially incorrect clocks
Spam floods Standard region and forwarding controls Block forwarding of identified spam floods to protect the repeater's transmit airtime
Diagnosing a problem Inspect individual statistics and counters Plain-language findings for battery, links, noise and interference, with a suggested next action
Direct-message delivery A missed hop can interrupt delivery 35% higher delivery with Direct Packet Retries enabled

* WiFi updates and firmware rollback require supported ESP32 builds.

Advanced configuration

The configurator exposes every internal setting, names a repeater, sets its region transport scopes, and pushes the lot over USB. Boards running MeshCore Enhanced+ expose more settings than stock does, including the sync overlay a repeater picks up over the air. The tool assumes familiarity with how the mesh is configured (a wrong value can quietly take a repeater off the air). This is not the tool for a first-time user, but for someone who knows MeshCore's internals and likes turning knobs.
